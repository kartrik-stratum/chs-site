Official Rules

Rules draft — client/legal to review

No purchase or payment of any kind is necessary to enter or win. A purchase will not increase your chances of winning. Void where prohibited.

These Official Rules govern any sweepstakes, giveaway or prize drawing run by Caliber Home Solutions (the “Promotion”), together with the prize, entry period and entry method stated in the advertisement for that Promotion.

  1. 1

    Sponsor

    Caliber Home Solutions, 6025 South Yellowstone Highway, Idaho Falls, Idaho (the “Sponsor”). The Promotion is not sponsored, endorsed or administered by, or associated with, Facebook, Instagram, Meta, Google or any other platform.

  2. 2

    Eligibility

    Open to legal residents of the United States, 18 or older at the time of entry, who live in the Sponsor’s service area unless a Promotion states otherwise. Employees of the Sponsor and their immediate family or household members are not eligible.

  3. 3

    Entry Period

    Each Promotion runs for the entry period stated in its advertisement. Entries received before or after that period are void. The Sponsor’s clock is the official timekeeper.

  4. 4

    How to Enter

    Complete the entry form for the Promotion with accurate contact information. One (1) entry per person unless stated otherwise. Automated, incomplete or fraudulent entries are void.

  5. 5

    Prize

    The prize, its approximate retail value (ARV) and number of prizes are as described in the Promotion’s advertisement. Prizes are non-transferable and have no cash alternative, except the Sponsor may substitute a prize of equal or greater value. Winners pay all applicable taxes.

  6. 6

    Odds & Winner Selection

    Odds depend on the number of eligible entries received. Unless a Promotion is judged, the winner is chosen in a random drawing from all eligible entries after the entry period closes.

  1. 7

    Winner Notification

    The potential winner is contacted using the details provided at entry. If they can’t be reached within five (5) business days, are ineligible or decline, an alternate winner may be selected.

  2. 8

    Publicity

    Where permitted by law, entry gives the Sponsor consent to use the winner’s name, city/state and likeness for advertising without further compensation.

  3. 9

    Privacy

    Information collected from entrants is used in line with our Privacy Policy. By entering, you agree that the Sponsor may contact you about the Promotion and its services.

  4. 10

    Limitation of Liability

    Entrants release the Sponsor and its affiliates from claims arising from participation or from the use of any prize. The Sponsor is not responsible for lost, late or misdirected entries.

  5. 11

    General Conditions

    The Sponsor may modify, suspend or cancel any Promotion, or disqualify anyone who tampers with entry. These Rules are governed by the laws of the State of Idaho.

  6. 12

    Requesting Rules or a Winners List

    For full details of an active Promotion, or the name of a winner, contact Caliber Home Solutions at (208) 552-3037. See also our Offer Terms.
