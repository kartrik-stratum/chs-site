About Us

Aging-in-Place Remodeling Services From Trusted, Local Professionals

At Caliber Home Solutions, our goal is to help as many people as possible remain in their homes. We understand the challenges of aging in place and use our 20+ years of experience to provide tailored home modification solutions that improve comfort, safety, and independence every day.

As an aging-in-place remodeling specialist, Caliber Home Solutions helps homeowners improve bathroom accessibility with walk-in tubs and walk-in showers designed to reduce the risk of slips and falls while providing a comfortable bathing experience. We also offer a range of home accessibility solutions, including stair lifts, home elevators, and wheelchair platform lifts. Our aging-in-place remodeling services are designed to help homeowners improve the functionality, safety, and comfort of their homes.
