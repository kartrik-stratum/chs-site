Walk-in Tub Safety Features

One of the key safety features of walk-in tubs is their low-threshold entry, which makes getting in and out easier. Unlike standard tubs and shower combinations, walk-in tubs reduce the need to step over a high tub wall, helping lower the risk of slips and falls. They also include features designed to make bathing safer and more comfortable, such as:
