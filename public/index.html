Frequently asked

The answers to common questions about installation, pricing, financing and our home accessibility services.

Still have a question?Real answers from a local team, today.(208) 552-3037
How long does a walk-in shower or tub installation take?

Installation timelines depend on the product, project scope and site conditions. Your Caliber consultant will provide an estimated timeline for your project, and many bathroom remodels are completed in as little as one day.

How much does aging-in-place remodeling cost?

Every home is different. After a free in-home consultation we give you clear, itemized pricing with no obligation.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. Flexible financing is available, including 0% APR options for qualified buyers.

Can a stair lift fit a curved staircase?

Yes. We install straight and custom curved-rail stair lifts. We measure your staircase during the consultation.

Do I need a shaft for a home elevator?

Not always. Through-floor home elevators such as Stiltz don't need a shaft or machine room, which makes installation simpler.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Idaho and the surrounding Mountain West, including Montana, Utah, Wyoming, Nevada, Oregon and Washington.
